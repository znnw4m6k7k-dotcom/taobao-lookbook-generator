# High-key minimalist studio style lock

Apply this lock to every classic studio shot. In `background-remix`, apply it only when the user requests this studio treatment or the supplied background is a seamless white/light cool-gray studio; never override a different supplied scene.

This is a photography and art-direction lock. It never overrides identity, product, outfit, or approved accessory truth. Mentions of knitwear, trousers, handbags, or shoes apply only when those items are actually present in the locked outfit.

## Background and aesthetic

- High-key European minimalist fashion lookbook with understated luxury and a quiet, premium independent-brand atmosphere.
- Seamless pure white to very light cool-gray studio background, clean, low contrast, and evenly illuminated.
- Keep subtle tonal information in the white background; do not clip it into featureless pure white.
- Use restrained neutral color grading, subtle 35mm film-scan character, and extremely fine natural grain.

## Lighting

- Use one very large, soft, diffused source resembling an oversized softbox or soft natural window light.
- Place the key approximately 30–45 degrees to the model's front-left and slightly above eye level.
- Light must be broad, even, and extremely soft, without hard directional shadows.
- Use natural white bounce fill from the opposite side to retain face, hair, garment, accessory, and shadow detail.
- Keep only a very subtle soft contact shadow beneath the feet.
- No obvious background gradient, dramatic rim light, commercial flash effect, or glossy advertising light.

## Camera and composition

- Camera height: approximately chest-to-waist level, mostly level and straight, without noticeable high- or low-angle distortion.
- Allowed views according to the assigned shot: straight front, 30–45-degree front three-quarter, 90-degree side profile, approximately 45-degree rear three-quarter, and straight back.
- Use a relatively long camera-to-subject distance to minimize perspective distortion.
- Preserve natural, subtly elongated proportions without exaggeration: no oversized head or feet, artificially stretched legs, or wide-angle distortion.
- Leave generous negative space around the head and body; keep the composition quiet and refined.
- Simulate a full-frame camera with a 70–85mm medium telephoto lens, preferably about 85mm, for natural perspective compression.

## Focus and exposure

- Aperture: approximately f/5.6–f/8. Do not simulate f/1.4 or f/2.
- Keep the subject consistently sharp from face, eyes, and hair through the complete outfit and visible accessories down to the shoes.
- Background softness must be extremely subtle and natural, never obvious bokeh.
- ISO 100–200; shutter approximately 1/125–1/200 second; white balance approximately 5200–5600K.
- Exposure is slightly bright with controlled highlight roll-off, gentle shadows, and preserved tonal separation.
- Use low-to-medium contrast and restrained saturation. Skin remains realistic, neutral, and slightly cool.
- Preserve visible fiber, folds, seams, surface texture, and tonal variation, especially in black garments and dark brown knitwear when present.

## Realism character

- Realistic skin texture and visible pores; real hair, anatomy, fabric behavior, and contact with the floor.
- Clean editorial/catalog photography rather than influencer, livestream, or mass-market commercial imagery.
- Soft diffused studio daylight, muted neutral tones, realistic fabric texture, and restrained retouching.

## Strict negative lock

Reject harsh studio flash, hard shadows, dramatic Rembrandt lighting, strong rim light, obvious background blur, creamy bokeh, wide-angle distortion, exaggerated proportions, unnaturally long legs, oversized head or feet, excessive skin smoothing, plastic skin, HDR, oversharpening, aggressive clarity, strong warm grading, high contrast, fully blown-out white background, artificial CGI appearance, glossy advertising lighting, influencer photography, and livestream e-commerce photography.
